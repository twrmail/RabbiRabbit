# bsb.txt: the publisher's file

The whole Berean Standard Bible as one text file, as published at https://bereanbible.com/bsb.txt and given to us by Tom Robinson on 2026-11-06 (saved from the browser and attached to a chat, so it is the copy he had, byte for byte).

- sha256: `beacd9018a81c368fd36ca254599e1a259a861863c5c767dbfd0ad378a29badb`
- Size: 4,300,284 bytes; UTF-8; one verse per line: reference, a tab, the verse text. Three header lines come first.
- Header lines: "The Holy Bible, Berean Standard Bible, BSB is produced in cooperation with Bible Hub, Discovery Bible, unfoldingWord, Bible Aquifer, OpenBible.com, and the Berean Bible Translation Committee." and "This text of God's Word has been dedicated to the public domain. Free resources and databases are available at BereanBible.com."
- 31,102 verse lines in 66 books. 16 verses have empty text (Matthew 17:21, 18:11, 23:14; Mark 7:16, 9:44, 9:46, 11:26, 15:28; Luke 17:36, 23:17; John 5:4; Acts 8:37, 15:34, 24:7, 28:29; Romans 16:24): verses the translation does not contain.

Do not edit this file. It is the reference the BSB files in library/ are checked against. `data/bsb/bsb.json` is the older converted copy (from seven1m/open-bibles) and is left as it is.
